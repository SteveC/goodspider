Last updated: June 3, 2026

GoodSpider builds and operates focused apps and web tools, including Airliners Encyclopedia. This policy explains how we handle information for Airliners Encyclopedia and related GoodSpider apps.

Information We Collect

Airliners Encyclopedia does not require you to create an account. The app does not sell personal information.

The app may process information needed to provide app features:

  • Search or chat prompts you enter in the app, if you use the Ask feature.
  • Purchase status information provided by Apple when you buy or restore an in-app subscription.
  • Basic device and network information that Apple or hosting providers may process to operate App Store, TestFlight, and app services.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate app features, provide subscription access, respond to support requests, and maintain the service.

In-App Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Airliners Encyclopedia does not receive or store your full payment card details.

Third-Party Services

The app may use Apple services for App Store, TestFlight, StoreKit, and in-app purchase processing. The Ask feature may send prompts to a backend service to generate answers.
