GoodSpider

Privacy Choices

GoodSpider’s focused encyclopedia apps do not require an account, sell personal information, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Optional AI Chat

Premium chat is optional. Questions, recent chat context, and relevant encyclopedia excerpts are sent for AI processing only when you use chat. You can avoid this processing by not using the chat feature.

Information on Your Device

Reading progress, favorites, preferences, and local aggregate feature counters are stored on your device. Clearing the App's data or deleting the App removes its locally stored information, subject to your Apple or Android device and backup settings. This does not delete server-stored conversations or cancel a subscription.

Aggregate Product Analytics

The App sends daily feature-use counts with the encyclopedia name and app version to help us improve the product. These analytics do not contain article titles, search terms, chat text, account identifiers, or device identifiers, and are not linked to your identity. Previously received aggregate counts cannot be associated with an individual installation for deletion.

Access or Delete Server Information

Email hello@goodspider.net with the subject “Encyclopedia privacy request” and name the encyclopedia you use to request access to or deletion of chat, entitlement or support information. Do not include sensitive information in your first message; do not send passwords, payment-card details, raw purchase tokens or session credentials.

Because the App does not create an account or collect your email address in the App, we may need information from your installation or purchase to identify the correct records and verify the request. Chat records are linked to a pseudonymous purchase-derived identifier. Some technical or aggregate records cannot be associated with a particular person. We may retain limited records where needed to provide purchased access, prevent fraud, maintain security or meet legal obligations.

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are managed by the store where you bought them. For Apple purchases, view, change, or cancel a subscription in your Apple account. For Android purchases, manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play, or open Google Play and choose Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the App or requesting deletion of server data does not cancel store billing.

For more detail, read the Privacy Policy.
