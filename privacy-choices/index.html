Privacy Choices

The Encyclopedia of Airliners does not require an account, sell personal information, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Optional AI Chat

Premium chat is optional. Questions, recent chat context, and relevant encyclopedia excerpts are sent for AI processing only when you use chat. You can avoid this processing by not using the chat feature.

Information on Your Device

Reading progress, favorites, preferences, and local aggregate feature counters are stored on your device. Deleting the App removes its locally stored information, subject to Apple’s device and backup behavior.

Access or Delete Server Information

Email hello@goodspider.net with the subject “Airliners privacy request” to request access to or deletion of chat or support information. Do not include sensitive information in your first message.

Because the App does not create an account or collect your email address in the App, we may need information from your installation to locate the correct records. We may be unable to associate anonymous technical records with a particular person.

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are managed by Apple. You can view, change, or cancel a subscription in your Apple account.

For more detail, read the Privacy Policy.
